epic_journal

This is a support page for The app Epic Journal. For Questions, Contact us on: benet255@gmail.com

The purpose of this app is to keep a daily journal, write every day. When the current day ends the days journal will be saved on the server and can be found on the saved journals page. From there you can then generate AI images in the form of manga and other styles. You can then create your own story with your life as base. Everything is easily downloaded to any device you want.
